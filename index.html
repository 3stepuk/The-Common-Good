How to trust it
This path follows the structure of the Compendium, chapter for chapter. Where it states doctrine, it states what the Church teaches. Where it makes a judgement about a particular situation, it says so, and a Catholic is free to disagree with that judgement. Nothing here has any authority of its own.
Not an official text. A study path, written by a man still learning it.
