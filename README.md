# The Common Good — twelve sessions

A path through Catholic social doctrine: the human person, the four principles,
the family, work, economic life, the political community, nations and migration,
creation, peace, and what it asks of you.

Built on the *Compendium of the Social Doctrine of the Church* (2004) and the
encyclicals from *Rerum Novarum* (1891) to *Fratelli Tutti* (2020), freely
available at vatican.va. Citations are to chapters and named documents.

Static, offline, no accounts, no tracking.
